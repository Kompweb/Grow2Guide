Do you hate the work, or the place you're doing it?

They're not the same thing, and the answer changes what you do next.

A healthy agency looks like: expectations that are the same for everyone, supervision that actually happens, policies leadership follows too, and bad news that's safe to bring.

Ask yourself: if you did this same job at a well-run agency, would you feel different?
