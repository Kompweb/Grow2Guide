The shift is over. You did what you could. And your brain is replaying it anyway.

Try three lines on paper:
1. What happened, and what I knew at the time.
2. Did I follow my process?
3. One thing I'd do differently next time.

Then close the notebook. A good process with a bad outcome is still good work.

And don't carry it alone: talk to your supervisor or a colleague you trust.
