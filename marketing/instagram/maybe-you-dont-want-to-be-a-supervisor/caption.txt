"Where do you see yourself in five years?" Everyone says: moving up.

But supervision asks you to disappoint people you like, hold expectations when it would be easier to be liked, and watch others do the work you love.

Before you apply, ask: what part of my job do I love most? Do I want the job, or the title and the pay? Both are fair. Just be honest.

Up isn't the only direction.
