Refunds

Last updated: September 2026

Digital PDF products are delivered by email after payment. Because files can be copied once delivered, refunds are reviewed case by case.

  • Refund requests for duplicate purchases or delivery failures are typically honored.
  • Requests after successful download may be declined once files have been delivered, except where required by law.

Consulting & mentoring

  • Deposits and session fees follow the written agreement for that engagement.

How to request

Email with your name, order details, and reason. We aim to respond within two business days.
