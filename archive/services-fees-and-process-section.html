Client experience

A seven-stage process makes complex work manageable

1

Fit check

A 20-minute call confirms the program, target authority, stage, timeline, decision-makers, and immediate risks. The call does not provide detailed regulatory advice.

2

Paid discovery

The Strategy Intensive or Readiness Assessment establishes the right pathway when scope is unclear.

3

Scope and agreement

Grow2Guide defines outcomes, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, timeline, client responsibilities, milestones, and fee.

4

Secure onboarding

The client names a project lead, provides requested materials, grants approved access, and confirms meeting cadence.

5

Assessment and roadmap

Requirements are mapped to current documents, workflows, staffing, evidence, and operational practice. Priorities are ranked by risk and dependency.

6

Build and implement

Grow2Guide drafts or refines agreed materials, coaches leaders, tracks decisions, and tests whether policies are being used in practice.

7

Readiness and closeout

A mock review, findings debrief, and sustainability plan identify remaining ownership, deadlines, and recommended ongoing support.
