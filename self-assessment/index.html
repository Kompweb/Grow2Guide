Care Competence & Workplace Competence Self-Assessment

Working in behavioral health requires more than the ability to care about people. It also requires professional judgment, accountability, communication, boundaries, and the ability to work effectively with others.

This self-assessment is designed to help you reflect on two related but distinct areas:

  • Care Competence: How you interact with and respond to the people you serve.
  • Workplace Competence: How you fulfill your professional responsibilities and work with colleagues, supervisors, and your organization.

Answer based on your actual behavior over the past 30 days, especially during stressful situations, disagreements, or moments when you felt frustrated or overwhelmed. Choose the response that best reflects what you typically do, not what you believe you should do.

This tool is intended for personal reflection and professional development. It is not a validated psychological test, a diagnostic tool, or a measure of your overall worth as a professional.
