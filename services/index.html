Client experience

A seven-stage process makes complex work manageable

1

Fit check

A 20-minute call confirms the program, target authority, stage, timeline, decision-makers, and immediate risks. The call does not provide detailed regulatory advice.

2

Paid discovery

The Strategy Intensive or Readiness Assessment establishes the right pathway when scope is unclear.

3

Scope and agreement

Grow2Guide defines outcomes, deliverables, assumptions, exclusions, timeline, client responsibilities, milestones, and fee.

4

Secure onboarding

The client names a project lead, provides requested materials, grants approved access, and confirms meeting cadence.

5

Assessment and roadmap

Requirements are mapped to current documents, workflows, staffing, evidence, and operational practice. Priorities are ranked by risk and dependency.

6

Build and implement

Grow2Guide drafts or refines agreed materials, coaches leaders, tracks decisions, and tests whether policies are being used in practice.

7

Readiness and closeout

A mock review, findings debrief, and sustainability plan identify remaining ownership, deadlines, and recommended ongoing support.

Commercial guardrails

Clear terms protect the client and Grow2Guide

Payment structure

  • Projects under $5,000: payment in full to schedule.
  • Projects from $5,000 to $15,000: 50% at signing, 50% at midpoint or delivery.
  • Projects over $15,000: 40% at signing, 30% at midpoint, 30% before final readiness review.
  • Retainers: billed monthly in advance by automatic payment.
  • Onsite work: travel estimate approved and paid before booking.

Standard boundaries

  • Client delays extend the timeline; expedited catch-up work may require a change order.
  • One consolidated client review round is included per major deliverable unless otherwise stated.
  • New locations, programs, service lines, applications, or authorities require written rescoping.
  • Grow2Guide provides consulting and implementation support, not legal advice or representation.
  • Licensure, certification, and accreditation decisions belong solely to the responsible authority; no outcome is guaranteed.
  • The client remains responsible for factual accuracy, approvals, implementation, staffing, training, and operating compliance.
  • Current standards and state instructions should be confirmed at kickoff and before submission.

Investment statement

Your investment is based on the agreed scope, current readiness, number of programs and locations, document volume, and target timeline. If discovery reveals a material change in scope, Grow2Guide will pause, explain the impact, and obtain written approval before additional work begins.

Outcome statement

Grow2Guide will provide practical guidance, deliverables, and readiness support aligned to the agreed requirements. Because regulators and accreditors retain independent decision-making authority, Grow2Guide cannot promise a license, certification, accreditation decision, or survey result.
