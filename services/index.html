Tier 2

Organizational Gap Assessment

You Have the Pieces. We'll Find What's Missing.

You provide your existing policies, procedures, forms, workflows, and organizational materials. We assess what you have—and how well it actually works in practice.

Because being compliant on paper isn't the same as being compliant in practice. Halfway to licensing or accreditation but feel stuck? We've got you.

We Assess

  • Where you stand against licensing and accreditation requirements
  • Whether policies reflect day-to-day operations
  • Whether systems are actually being implemented
  • Readiness across inpatient and outpatient programs
  • Documentation, workflow, or compliance gaps that could affect a survey
  • Licensing, accreditation, audit, and operational risks
  • What to prioritize before your next survey or renewal

Our assessments may consider applicable state licensing, health department requirements, adult and aging services, CARF, The Joint Commission, regulatory audits, payer reviews, and other oversight requirements.

Become & Stay Ready

Don't Have the Infrastructure? We'll Build It—and Show You How to Use It.

Starting or rebuilding a behavioral health or aging-services organization requires more than policies on paper.

We build the infrastructure—and help you put it into practice.

We Build

  • Licensing and accreditation readiness, from the ground up
  • Policies & procedures
  • Program manuals
  • Inpatient and outpatient programs, across different levels of care
  • Forms & documentation
  • Clinical & operational workflows
  • QA/QI systems
  • Compliance & risk-management systems
  • Staff training & competency structures
  • Audit and monitoring systems

We Implement

We don't simply hand you a manual.

We help you translate policies into day-to-day practice, show your team how systems should work, establish implementation processes, and create structures for ongoing monitoring and accountability.

Our work can support readiness for applicable state licensing, health department requirements, adult and aging services, CARF, The Joint Commission, regulatory audits, payer reviews, and other oversight requirements.
